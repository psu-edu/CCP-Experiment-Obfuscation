Welcome to the Digital Records of the Colored Conventions Project!

This site, omeka.coloredconventions.org, is the Colored Conventions Project's (CCP) digital records. This site features the hundreds of collected documents of the Colored Conventions movement, spanning from the 1830s through the 1890s.
